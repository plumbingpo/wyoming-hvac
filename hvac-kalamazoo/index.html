Keeping Your Kalamazoo HVAC System Running Longer

Routine maintenance is the difference between a system that lasts and one that quits in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

How old is your system? Its age tells you a lot. Most well-maintained air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that range and is also showing higher bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement when the first heat wave hits.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Take a weak capacitor: replacing it is a short, affordable repair, but left alone for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a miserable night.
